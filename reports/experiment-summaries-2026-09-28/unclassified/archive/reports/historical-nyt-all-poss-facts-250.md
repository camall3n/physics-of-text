No contemporaneous complete semantic annotations or precision/recall score are saved in this directory. The existing comparison's 123/250 multiset intersection with the full 2026 NYT TSV is corpus overlap, not fact precision, recall or partition agreement. The complete triple display supports later reconstruction, subject to repeated-row ambiguity and archived entity/score limitations.

The later [September-14 supplemental census](../../../../../experiments/nyt-complete-evaluation-2026-09-14/supplemental/audit_archive250/assessment.md) reconstructed and evaluated all **223 expressed facts in all 15 relations** of this saved MAP (250/250 rows): 140 supported, 54 incorrect, 29 ambiguous, **62.78–75.78%**, decided-only 72.16% at 87.00% decided coverage, zero human overrides. This is a full expressed-fact census for this different 250-row corpus, not a sample and not the full NYT top-20 experiment. It assigns the broad `managerial_office_in` predicate to all 15 relations; its score does not mean 15 distinct precise titles were recovered. Counts are conditional on that predicate scope and assistant judgments, not confidence limits or gold recall. This is a derived later assessment, not new inference.
