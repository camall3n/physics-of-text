Predicate ID: lobbyist_for

Person or organization X holds or held an explicitly identified lobbying role for principal Y.

Includes: explicit lobbyist for or of Y; historical lobbying service for Y. Excludes: director of legislation or another managerial title without a lobbying role; ordinary advocacy, communication, legal service, or political agreement alone; membership in the principal organization alone.
