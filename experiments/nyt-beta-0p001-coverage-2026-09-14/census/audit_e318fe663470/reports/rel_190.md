# audit_e318fe663470 — rel_190: succeeded person in role

Predicate ID: succeeded_person

Person X took over a role previously held by person Y.

Includes: explicit succession or replacement in an office or professional role; historical successor taking office; clearly established temporary or acting replacement. Excludes: X appointed a third person to replace Y; generic opposition, criticism, or substitution unrelated to a role; a proposed successor without established tenure. Ambiguous unless resolved by case-local evidence: bare replace where appointing versus taking over is unresolved; unclear predecessor or role; candidate or announced future replacement without tenure. Direction is successor to predecessor. It does not imply that X and Y are the same person or held office simultaneously.
