# audit_e318fe663470 — rel_346: fielded or used an athlete in play

Predicate ID: fielded_player

Team, coach, or manager X fields or uses athlete Y in sporting play.

Includes: explicit playing or fielding of an individual athlete by a team or coach; historical use of the athlete in play. Excludes: playing against an opposing team or competitor; mere player membership without X selecting or using the athlete; ordinary employment or political candidacy. Ambiguous unless resolved by case-local evidence: generic removal without a clear sporting selection context; play whose athlete-versus-opponent role is unresolved; prospective selection or materially incomplete action roles. The subject can be the coach rather than the team. Bare play is polysemous; inspect both arguments and all local evidence. Removing an athlete does not by itself establish that X fielded the athlete.

Complete census: 2 supported, 6 incorrect, 1 ambiguous; N=9. Precision 2/9=22.22% to 3/9=33.33%.
