Predicate ID: politically_controlled_by

Political institution, legislature, government, or geographic territory X is or was politically controlled by group or government Y.

Includes: explicit political control, takeover, or governing majority by Y; historical control including clearly established loss of prior control. Excludes: an ordinary member or individual officeholder without group control; corporate ownership; political support or influence alone; proposed takeover without realization. Ambiguous unless resolved by case-local evidence: generic have, take, or govern without clear control context; unclear political body or group identity; control versus ordinary office unresolved. Inverse of politically_controls. A mere member of a legislature does not establish party control.
