Predicate ID: publisher_of

Includes: explicit publisher-of title; explicit publication of the identified newspaper, journal, book, or other work; historical publisher role. Excludes: editor, writer, owner, developer, or director alone; travel to a publisher or publication office; a work merely mentioning or reviewing Y.
