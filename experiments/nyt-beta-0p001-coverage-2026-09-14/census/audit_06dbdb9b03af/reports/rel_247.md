Predicate ID: calls_up_players_from

Sports team X calls up, promotes, recalls, or summons players from source team or affiliate Y.

Includes: explicit promotion or recall from a source team; calling or summoning a player from Y into X; clear team-to-affiliate recall or promotion context even if the player's name is omitted. Excludes: ordinary travel or communication from a geographic place; corporate ownership alone; sending a player in the opposite direction; a proposed promotion without realization. Ambiguous unless resolved by case-local evidence: the omitted object could be something other than a player; unclear source versus destination team; bare call-from without recruitment context. The direction is receiving team to source team. It does not require ownership of the affiliate or imply that all its players move.
