Predicate ID: location_of_organization

Geographic place X hosts the physical location, base, or office of organization Y.

Includes: explicit organization location, headquarters, office, or base in X; a geographically identified office or division of Y located in X; historical physical organizational location. Excludes: X is a corporate subsidiary or parent rather than a geographic place; national affiliation alone without physical location; ordinary travel, discussion, or an unrelated event in X. Ambiguous unless resolved by case-local evidence: place versus institution metonymy unresolved; a geographic division name without clear physical location; unclear organization or location attachment. This is the inverse direction of located_in. The presence of an office does not imply that all of Y is headquartered there; corporate ownership alone is not location.
