Predicate ID: publisher_of

Person or organization X holds or held the publisher role for publication Y, or explicitly publishes Y.

Includes: explicit publisher-of title; explicit publication of the identified newspaper, journal, book, or other work; historical publisher role. Excludes: editor, writer, owner, developer, or director alone; travel to a publisher or publication office; a work merely mentioning or reviewing Y. Ambiguous unless resolved by case-local evidence: incomplete publication title; unclear publisher-versus-author or title attachment. Publisher is distinct from editor and from ownership. Co-occurring ownership does not replace the requirement for publication or an explicit publisher role.
