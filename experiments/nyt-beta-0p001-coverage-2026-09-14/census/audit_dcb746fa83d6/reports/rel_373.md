Predicate ID: fought_war_against

Political entity or military group X fought an armed conflict or war against Y.

Includes: explicit war-with or fought-against in an armed-conflict context; actual military attack or combat against Y; historical military adversaries. Excludes: sports competition or metaphorical political attack; cooperation, alliance, or joint action on the same side; border adjacency or diplomatic relations alone; planned conflict without realization. Ambiguous unless resolved by case-local evidence: fight-with whose allied versus adversarial sense is unresolved; generic attack without military context; future threat or proposed war. Military adversarial conflict is distinct from cooperation and from criticizing. The predicate does not imply a winner.
