Predicate ID: acted_jointly_with

Entity X explicitly acted jointly or cooperated with counterpart Y in a shared action.

Includes: explicit cooperation or joint action; joining Y in a specified shared action; historical realized cooperation. Excludes: joining an institution as a member; physical arrival, river confluence, or ordinary accompaniment; a generic relationship without a shared action; fighting against Y. Ambiguous unless resolved by case-local evidence: join alone with no shared action supplied; plans or proposed cooperation without realization; unclear cooperative versus adversarial action. A narrow joint-action meaning, not a generic association. With truncated join-only paths and no action context, use A rather than assuming what the entities did together.
