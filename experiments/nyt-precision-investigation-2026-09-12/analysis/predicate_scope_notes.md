# Predicate scope and reviewer caveats

Relation discovery requires choosing what one cluster means before judging its facts. This can materially affect a score when a dictionary combines different meanings. The records below preserve all declared labels, definitions and scope notes, including competing interpretations; they are not an instruction to credit their union. Multiple assistant reviewers used the same protocol but have no independently calibrated human agreement score. A reviewer’s choice among near-tied meanings is a source of uncertainty beyond random sampling and the S/E/A endpoints.
