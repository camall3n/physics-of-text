Separate logs support the enumerated relation, entity-partition and new-move claims. These provide bounded evidence; the README does not characterize them as a proof for every possible state or claim that full NYT runs were replayed.

## Wording issue resolved

An earlier generated results paragraph claimed that positive differences between the final two block means showed that the chains were still searching. A stationary correlated chain can also produce such a difference. The generator and report now say that these differences are consistent with movement toward higher-density regions, do not prove nonstationarity, and do not establish convergence. The revised wording is appropriate.

Hash verification covers the explicitly fingerprinted originals and recorded outputs, not an assertion about every file elsewhere in the repository.
