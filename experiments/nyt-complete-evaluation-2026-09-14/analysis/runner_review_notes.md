# Runner review and reporting notes

Complete dictionaries and supplied case rows were manually reviewed. Current worklists contain only retained evaluations; earlier workload totals included retired active-defect runs. The exact worklist is [runner_catalogue_worklist.json](runner_catalogue_worklist.json); the coverage check is [runner_completion.json](runner_completion.json). Per-relation reading/decision records are in [manual_records](manual_records/). No keyword classifier or extrapolation assigned semantic judgments.

The common catalogue controls scope. In particular, the retained president-or-manager predicate is composite: manager-only support is tagged `broad_predicate`. Chair, executive and general leadership alone do not satisfy it. Academic, economist, analyst, legal and spokesperson roles remain distinct. A historical explicit role can support a fact even when other rows express another relationship.

For directed communication, an addressee must be established. Telling, asking, urging, testimony, an addressed letter and an actual conversation provide support. Mere meetings or speech about a person do not. Bare send, submit or give paths can omit the object; these remain ambiguous when no other local evidence establishes communication. An institution can speak through an identified representative; attribution of an envoy's or administration's action to a particular person is explicitly questioned when that is the only support.

Different literal people or institutions inside one latent tuple are treated as unresolved local identity when they affect otherwise supportive evidence. Shared IDs do not justify collapsing them semantically. [self_entity_collision_flags.json](self_entity_collision_flags.json) additionally identifies same-ID argument positions with incompatible literal names; it is diagnostic only, without automatic labels.

The reporter produces [prior_comparison.md](../prior_comparison.md), [prior_comparison.json](../prior_comparison.json), and [judgment_changes.md](../judgment_changes.md). The first numerical step rejudges exactly the old cases with the original relation-population weights; the second expands to every fact using the current scope. This separates same-case revision from coverage expansion arithmetically, without claiming the cause of each revision. The retained old endpoint estimates are reconstructed from the preserved evidence/annotation snapshots and checked against their saved reports: [prior_metric_reconstruction.json](prior_metric_reconstruction.json).

Final reporting validates complete sources, cases, canonical predicates, citations and overrides for the ten retained main runs. All 32 infrastructure tests pass in [latest_test_results.txt](../tests/latest_test_results.txt). Preparation was rerun successfully after the navigation update; annotations and editable overrides are never overwritten. Every per-audit README now prominently links to the evaluated assessment and per-fact reports, and ambiguity links jump directly to case headings.
