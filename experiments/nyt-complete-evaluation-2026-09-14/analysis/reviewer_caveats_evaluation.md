# Leadership and office census: reviewer notes

The evaluation reviewer read complete dictionaries and supplied case-local evidence. Current worklists contain only retained evaluations; earlier workload totals included retired active-defect runs. Explicit index-to-case judgment records are in `analysis/manual_records/`; the reviewable final labels, reasons, questions, and source-line citations are in each census folder’s `annotations/rel_*.json`. The shared catalogue and METHOD govern these judgments. These are assistant semantic assessments, not independent historical verification or a calibrated human gold standard.

## Scope is part of the measurement

The inherited operational meanings were harmonized before this complete review. `managerial_office_in` accepts an explicit director, executive, president, manager, head, chief, or chair office, including a departmental office. It does not claim that every person is the sole head of the institution. `organizational_leader_of` retains the earlier narrower leader/head/president/chief/chair meaning; an otherwise unspecified director or executive is ambiguous there. `director_of` and `chairperson_of` are specific-title predicates. A supported director fact in the broad office family is therefore not automatically supported in a chairman-only cluster.

This distinction is consequential. Several relations combine director, spokesperson, economist, and president paths. A spokesperson or economist row alone is unsupported under the office definition. A fact with both an explicit directorship and spokesperson rows is supported and flagged as mixed evidence. For example, audit_e4fd4a14b8e2/rel_381 contains many such facts. The aggregate measures whether a fact has textual support for the declared office, not whether all of the relation’s assigned sentences express one exact title. Broad managerial-family precision and exact-title purity answer different questions.

The family is inherited, not a post hoc union chosen to rescue errors. Founders, owners, ordinary employees, members, advisers, analysts, lawyers, editors, and spokespersons do not count solely through those roles. Explicit editor-in-chief is an office; editor alone is not. Similarly, a majority/minority legislative leader or whip is leadership, while ordinary legislative membership is not. The narrower organizational-leadership definition remains distinct from the separate sports membership predicate.

## Identity and extraction boundaries

Case-local incompatible names prevent a unique supported entity pair. Examples include Powell and Myers sharing the subject of a Joint Chiefs fact, or an unrelated birth/death pair sharing the same latent fact as a corporate executive. These are ambiguous when a supporting office path exists. If every local row already fails the predicate, the case remains unsupported rather than being upgraded to ambiguity merely because it also has an entity collision. Global entity merges elsewhere in the world are reported separately and do not automatically invalidate a locally coherent fact. The metric therefore does not certify the entire inferred entity partition.

Truncated political adjectives are handled consistently as ambiguous when an office is otherwise supported: Democratic, Palestinian, Soviet, Yugoslav, and singular Bosnian Serb lack the complete institutional/group argument. Plural Bosnian Serbs can identify a group, and Republican can name the party when it is the institutional object of a named person’s leadership. When Republican or Democrat instead stands in for an unnamed individual committee chair, the missing person is ambiguous. This surface distinction is an operational annotation boundary, not evidence that one political party has better inferred relations.

Conventional institutional short names, such as Central Intelligence, District Council, the Fed, and the Met/Metropolitan in museum context, are accepted when the local evidence identifies the institution. Pure fragments such as Modern, Taxi, Environment, or Study of American Catholicism are left ambiguous. This boundary remains open to user adjudication and should not be silently adjusted for one run. A director-for-office path can identify the institution explicitly: Ellen Borakove→Medical Examiner is supported when the path supplies the Medical Examiner’s **office**, whereas a director attached solely to Mr. Pataki, Mr. Bush, Mr. Clinton, or Mayor remains ambiguous about its omitted institution/campaign.

## Attachment, tenure, and direction

An office word somewhere in a long path is insufficient. Ted Turner→CNN with founder wording plus a chairman clause routed through a merger is ambiguous about which body the chairmanship concerns; founder-only cases remain unsupported. Mustafa Kemal Ataturk→Turkey with founder/transform wording is unsupported when the generic leader modifier does not attach an office to Turkey. E. Virgil Conway→M.T.A. with only a date/bill/chairman path is ambiguous; another case containing a direct chairman-of row is supported. This is why different evidence subsets for the same names need not receive the same judgment.

Historical offices are included. A clear current or former office can support a fact even when another row concerns a nomination or succession. A nomination-only or president-elect-only fragment is not sufficient proof that the office was held; unresolved tenure is ambiguous. Ordinary candidacy alone is unsupported.

`has_political_leader` runs from body to person. An unspecified minister alone is ambiguous under its inherited narrower scope, unless another local row establishes political leadership. `has_organizational_leader` separately preserves the broader inverse office meaning where originally declared; it includes a team president. `politically_controls` requires group control rather than a person’s office or generic electoral success. Thus plural Democrats→White House with an explicit control row is supported, whereas a singular unnamed Democrat merely winning/occupying the White House is not evidence of the group-control predicate.

## Limits of consistency checking

Each decision was made from the full dictionary and every supplied case row, then recorded with explicit indices and case IDs. The recording helper copies citations and checks coverage; it does not classify semantic evidence. Repeated complete evidence under the same predicate can be compared mechanically for disagreement, but that check cannot validate a shared mistaken interpretation. Nonidentical evidence subsets can legitimately differ, and predicate scopes are intentionally distinct. User corrections should retain the original judgment and record the adjudication so that all equivalent cases can be reconsidered together.
