# Complete, comparable NYT top-20 evaluation

This evaluation replaces the five-facts-per-relation screening design with a census. It evaluates the saved results; it does not rerun inference or change model parameters. Raw experiments are preserved. Earlier judgments remain for retained evaluations; active-defect grading records were retired under the [cleanup decision](../../reports/buggy-evaluation-cleanup-2026-09-28/README.md).

## Population and ranking

The main comparison includes ten retained full-NYT MAP worlds: eight September 12 investigation runs using corrected entity inference or fixed names, and two September 14 runs with corrected entity inference and lower dictionary smoothing. Each world contains the same 8,516 supplied dependency triples. Review every expressed fact in the 20 relations with the largest assigned sentence counts. Break count ties by numeric relation ID. Do not select a favorable checkpoint, change the ranking, or limit the number of facts per relation.

An evaluation unit is an ordered `(relation ID, first entity ID, second entity ID)` tuple within one saved world. Different latent facts remain separate even when their surface names match. Repeated sentence evidence for one fact does not add evaluation units. IDs carry no meaning across runs. The complete main population is 6,905 run-specific facts; that is not 6,905 independent real-world propositions.

All facts outside the top 20 are outside the primary precision denominator, just as in the earlier full evaluations. Unexpressed facts cannot be recovered from the sentence TSV and are also outside this denominator. Archived outputs that lack a complete comparable MAP world are documented separately; their missing facts must not be silently treated as errors or successes.

## Predicate declarations

Read the complete relation dictionary before assigning its directional meaning. Use the shared predicate catalogue and record the relation-to-predicate assignment. Preserve the scopes used by the earlier complete census where they are identifiable; document and reconcile differing scopes across later reviews. A narrower title and a broader organizational leadership office are different predicates, not interchangeable labels. Do not broaden a predicate after seeing errors to improve its score.

A review that changes an inherited definition must reconsider every affected fact, including previously sampled cases. Old judgments can be reused only with their source and case provenance retained and explicit confirmation that they fit the common definition and rubric. Matching a relation number in another run is never sufficient.

## Full case review

Read every fact and all its supplied name/path evidence. Assign exactly one judgment:

- **S — supported:** at least one supplied triple clearly supports the declared predicate for the ordered entities. Cite the supporting evidence. This establishes textual support, not independently checked historical truth.
- **E — unsupported (stored as `incorrect`):** the evidence clearly expresses another predicate, reverses the required direction, or has incompatible roles, and provides no clear support for the declared predicate. This does not assert that the newspaper sentence itself was false.
- **A — ambiguous:** the available triples leave a material uncertainty about identity, attachment, argument scope, modality, time, or predicate interpretation. Give a specific question for the user to resolve.

A supported fact can contain additional rows expressing unrelated predicates. Mark such cases with `mixed_evidence`; do not confuse fact support with sentence-assignment purity. Different historical outcomes can coexist: one clear victory can support an existential defeat fact even if another row records a loss.

Consider the identities of both arguments. Materially incompatible names *within the same fact* require explicit adjudication and normally an identity ambiguity when a supporting path exists but the intended entity pair is unresolved. Ordinary aliases are not automatically errors. Multiple names attached to an entity elsewhere in the world are useful diagnostic flags, but do not automatically overturn a fact-local judgment. Consequently this primary metric is not a measure of the correctness of the entire inferred entity partition.

Extraction, copying exact evidence, validation, and arithmetic may be automated. Semantic judgments must not be generated by a keyword classifier or inferred from a previous five-case sample. If an identical evidence case is reused under the same predicate, retain explicit provenance and verify identity and scope equivalence. Every final case needs a judgment, a reason, and valid case-local citations. No blank or merely provisional judgment counts as reviewed.

These are assistant judgments submitted for user inspection, not an independently calibrated human evaluation. The reviewers know the purpose of the investigation; opaque folder names do not establish a blinded study. Preserve original assistant judgments when applying human corrections.

## Exact census metrics

For a run, let `N = S + E + A` over its complete top-20 fact population. Report:

- Strict supported fraction: `S / N`.
- Ambiguity treated as support: `(S + A) / N`.
- Decided-only fraction: `S / (S + E)`, with decided coverage `(S + E) / N`.
- Per-relation counts and the unweighted mean of the within-relation fractions as a separate macro statistic.
- Top-20 sentence coverage, total expressed relations, and total expressed facts, to expose changes in what the top 20 select.

Each fact has equal weight in the primary aggregate. There is no subsampling, no inverse sampling weight, and no sampling confidence interval. The two primary endpoints show how unresolved cases affect the result; they are not confidence limits. A full census removes sampling uncertainty about this saved population, but not annotation uncertainty, differences between seeds, or uncertainty about the paper's undisclosed evaluation rules.

Runs remain separate. Their selected relations and populations differ, so the comparison is descriptive and unpaired. Do not pool seeds as independent corpus observations, equate top-20 coverage with recall, or claim an exact replication of the paper's reported precision without its original annotation criteria and judgments.

## Reproduction and user corrections

The census preparer copies and hashes every source, reconstructs every population, and preserves existing annotations. The reporter must reject incomplete coverage, duplicate or foreign cases, invalid citations, changed source evidence, and unconfirmed provisional imports. Reports show the judgment beside each fact, its predicate, all evidence, and any ambiguity question.

Use the separate human-review records to correct judgments; generated Markdown is a view, not the decision store. Rebuilding reports must preserve these records. The experiment README documents commands and the complete run inventory. Protected-file verification accounts for explicitly recorded authorized maintenance, including retired evaluation files.
